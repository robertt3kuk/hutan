## Result

**The release desktop is not generally CPU-bound when idle. The clearest remaining interactive cost is whole-window layout/rebuilding, including unrelated sidebar/history content.** Under flood, the daemon saturates one core doing terminal parsing and scrollback conversion, but the measured echo round trip remains sub-millisecond; the slow end-to-end typing measurement is overwhelmingly waiting for presentation.

No source files changed or commits made. Built release binaries, including symbolized release binaries. All started desktops, daemons, terminal children and benchmark processes were stopped; final process snapshot found none remaining.

## Ranked causes and fixes

### 1. Whole-window render/layout couples otherwise independent surfaces — confirmed

`crates/desktop/src/workbench/render.rs:70–104` renders all docks, center, titlebar, status and overlays on every root frame. There are no cached-view boundaries in that path. `crates/desktop/src/frames.rs:3–7` explicitly describes the immediate-mode whole-window measurement.

**Sample evidence:** `flood-symbolized-desktop.sample` has approximately **161 inclusive samples in separate `Window::compute_layout` branches versus 490 in `Window::draw` branches**, roughly one third of sampled drawing work. Representative stack:

```text
gpui_macos::window::step
  gpui::Window::draw
    ... layout_as_root
      gpui::Window::compute_layout
        taffy ... compute_child_layout / compute_preliminary
```

More importantly, while only filtering the **left** sessions list, `sidebar-symbolized-desktop.sample:277–293` shows the **right History list** rendering too:

```text
gpui::list::StateInner::layout_items
  HistoryPanel::render_entry
    HistoryPanel::render_row
      SessionItem::clone
      localtime_r / hutan_client::time::format_at
```

This is real cross-surface work, not just hypothetical immediate-mode overhead.

**Fix:** establish audited cached-view boundaries around docks, terminal/chat views and stable chrome; invalidate the affected panel instead of rebuilding every surface. Audit focus, active-window and hover dependencies before applying `AnyView::cached`.

**Expected gain [INFERENCE]:** substantial headroom in the **2.4–5.1 ms** normal frame work, with approximately **20–30% drawing-work reduction** a reasonable target from the sampled layout share—not a measured promised speedup. It should also make terminal floods less likely to disturb sidebar interaction.

### 2. History performs avoidable per-row cloning and formatting on unrelated frames — confirmed

`crates/desktop/src/workbench/panels/history_view.rs:1041` clones an entire `SessionItem` when rendering a row. Its fields include strings, latest turns, resume argv and evidence: `history_catalog.rs:63–84`.

The row also obtains home/time/settings at `history_view.rs:1052–1058`, constructs time tooltips at `:1203`, and formats metadata at `:1215`. Symbolized profiles directly contain the clone, vector/string cloning, `localtime_r`, and formatting calls.

**Fix:** retain shared immutable row data; cache presentation strings/tooltips by item revision and age bucket; hoist panel-invariant values out of each row render. Combine with the isolation in item 1.

**Expected gain [INFERENCE]:** modest alone—these are not the dominant samples—but eliminates repeated allocations and local-time work from terminal floods, hovers and left-sidebar typing. Do not present this as an indexing rewrite or a large standalone speedup.

### 3. Flood processing spends significant time converting scrollback and scanning input repeatedly — confirmed throughput cost, not proven input-lag cause

`flood-symbolized-daemon.sample:236–396`:

```text
hutan-term-read
  Emulator::feed                      3,122 samples in main branch
    Emulator::advance
      vte parser                      2,142 samples
    Emulator::settle
      RowScratch::fill                   903 samples in main settle branch
```

Additional `feed` branches account for **181 samples at `emulator.rs:316`** and **260 at `:323`**.

Source:
- `crates/terminal/src/emulator.rs:307–323`: separate sniffer, cell-size-query and clear-scanner passes.
- `:707–710`: parser advancement followed by settling for every slice.
- `:1008–1022`: spill converts history rows cell-by-cell into cold storage and trims the grid.

**Fix:** profile a consolidated/state-aware scan path, and optimize row conversion/spill without weakening bounded scrollback. Preserve escape-sequence ordering and resize/history invariants.

**Expected gain [INFERENCE]:** reducing spill and redundant scan costs offers material flood-throughput improvement; around **a quarter of reader samples** are in the principal settle/spill branch. **It is not the first responsiveness fix:** B5 daemon echo p95 is already **0.128 ms**, despite daemon CPU saturation.

### 4. Metadata invalidation is too broad — source-confirmed, contribution not isolated

`SessionStore::apply_state`, `crates/desktop/src/sessions/store.rs:276–334`, scans session/agent state, updates every attached terminal’s metadata and always notifies.

That notification fans out:

- Sessions panel increments a global store revision and notifies: `panels/sessions.rs:241–244`.
- The revision invalidates its entire entries cache: `:620–638`.
- History always notifies even when connection and scope did not change: `panels/history.rs:194–213`.
- Workbench invokes its session-change work and notifies: `workbench/terminals.rs:735–748`.

**Fix:** granular collection/session/scope revisions and notifications; rebuild the sidebar’s structural entries only when their inputs changed, not for every store notification.

**Expected gain [INFERENCE]:** primarily during many-agent metadata/status churn; no defensible numerical gain from these runs. **Do not claim every terminal-output packet traverses this path**: terminal renders have a separate delivery path.

### 5. Continuous display-link wakeups explain the idle CPU floor — confirmed power cost, not the main lag

`shown-desktop.sample:497–530` and symbolized profiles contain:

```text
CVDisplayLink::runIOThread
  CVDisplayLink::performIO
    gpui_macos::display_link::display_link_output_callback
      dispatch_source_merge_data
```

Pinned GPUI:
- `gpui_macos/src/window.rs:836–855`: starts display link while visible.
- `gpui_macos/src/display_link.rs:328–343`: subscribes until stopped.
- `gpui_macos/src/window.rs:3342–3362`: dispatches the frame callback.

**Fix:** demand-driven macOS frame wakeup, stopping the display subscription while no frame/animation is wanted.

**Expected gain [INFERENCE]:** remove most of the approximately **0.5%** visible-idle floor and unnecessary wakeups. This is battery/idle quality, not a likely explanation for perceptible list lag.

## Important presentation finding

The B5 failure is almost entirely **echo waiting to reach a frame**, not handler, socket or daemon processing. Its approximately **55 ms** p95 and **20 paints/s** should not trigger a daemon rewrite.

Pinned GPUI also has inactive-window throttling at `gpui/src/window.rs:1813–1847`. **Whether that caused this particular B5 result remains [INFERENCE]**: the trace records active at benchmark start, but does not establish continuous activity/visibility for the whole flood. Other scenarios demonstrably encountered occlusion. First distinguish presentation/throttling from frame work; retain both metrics. `frames.rs:20–22` correctly distinguishes Hutan work from display wait.

## Things not supported as current causes

- **“Sidebar builds all 300 rows every frame” is outdated.** It uses `gpui::list` at `panels/sessions.rs:989` and cached entries at `:620–638`. History is virtualized too: `history_view.rs:935–938`.
- History timers are **30 s refresh**, **700 ms scan polling**, **250 ms search debounce**: `history_catalog.rs:20–24`; hidden refreshes are gated at `history.rs:218–223`. The small synthetic indexing test did not show a CPU storm.
- Logging was negligible: every manual daemon stdout log was **0 bytes**; desktop logs **428 bytes** each.
- These were release builds. `Cargo.toml:98–101` uses thin LTO and one codegen unit; no evidence that debug assertions explain these measurements.
